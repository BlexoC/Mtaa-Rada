# OfflineNavigation App for Nairobi

# ProblemStatement and Proposed Solution

By Blessing Makau

## Problem Statement

Navigationin Nairobi is very hectic. A lot of people rely on strangers to givedirections to stages, buildings, offices, shopping centers andamusement parks.

It is not as though nobody has tried to solve this before. There havebeen funded projects, solo projects and school projects.

```
Most  of them depend on internet and have patchy stage data, whereas mineworks fully offline and guides you by voice.
```

## ProposedSolution

My solution comes in handy with offline navigation, not only to stagesbut also to other important locations like clinics and stores. Ittells you everything about that stop or location, with voice navigation. The cherry on top is that all of this can be done inoffline mode.

Justopening the app is all you need. You can start conversing from theword go. It will be based mainly in the Nairobi area.

Thiswill be very cost effective. It will also prevent aimlessmisdirection for someone who is concerned about theft, and it makesnavigation easier than before.

## Future Enhancements

Later additions can include transport fares to locations, weatherconditions, or additional voice features.
